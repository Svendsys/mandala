# Implement

You have been handed a single GitHub issue that should be well-defined, and ready to be implemented.
Your job is not to blindly follow instructions, but to exercise a sincere best-effort mandate to do the work that
the issue calls for.

### 1. Ensure the task is clear
What the issue says you must do, and what the creator of the issue intended are not the same things.
An issue may contain outdated assumptions, it may lack important details, if the issue and its purpose is not clear
then you must raise this concern up the chain of command. 

### 2. Ground yourself
Before doing anything else, make sure you understand the purpose of the repository, its conventions and philosophies.
The issue you are implementing does not exist in isolation, but is part of a larger whole. Your job is first and foremost
to contribute to the development of that larger whole, on its terms. 

### 3. Clarify the possible paths forward
There may be several ways that this task can be solved. If so, make them explicit for yourself, and then evaluate which
path forward best suits the project philosophies and conventions. 

### 4. Create a plan 
Create a concrete plan for how to proceed with your chosen path forward, including how to thoroughly test your solution.

### 5. Execute your plan
Follow your plan to completion. If there are complications on the way that your plan didn't account for, then go back
to the plan and revise it before continuing. Ensure that the plan still makes sense rather than doubling down on a
flawed plan. 

### 6. Wrap up
Once the implementation is ready and tested, create a PR against the default branch. Subscribe to events on the PR.

### 7. Follow up
Use the /address-pr-review skill to address the reviews on your PR. You will be addressing reviews until you are explicitly
given permission to merge from your overseer, or the PR is merged by a human.
